Role Overview

We are seeking an experienced SEO Manager with a strong focus on Local Search and Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization. This role is responsible for driving local visibility, increasing map pack rankings, and generating qualified leads through strategic local SEO initiatives.

The ideal candidate understands how to turn local search intent into measurable business outcomes and has hands-on experience optimizing multi-location businesses.

Key Responsibilities

Google Business Profile Optimization

Manage and optimize Google Business Profiles across single and multi-location businesses
Improve rankings in the Google Map Pack through strategic updates and ongoing optimization
Develop and implement posting strategies, service updates, and product enhancements
Monitor and respond to reviews, and implement review generation strategies
Identify and resolve listing issues, suspensions, and inconsistencies
Local SEO Strategy

Develop and execute local SEO strategies to improve visibility in targeted geographic markets
Conduct local keyword research and map keywords to service/location pages
Optimize location landing pages for search intent, conversion, and relevance
Manage NAP consistency and local citations across directories
Build and manage local backlink strategies
Technical & On-Page SEO

Optimize on-page elements including metadata, schema (LocalBusiness), and internal linking
Collaborate with developers on technical SEO improvements (site speed, mobile usability, indexing)
Ensure proper tracking, tagging, and attribution for local campaigns
Analytics & Reporting

Track and report on key KPIs including rankings, traffic, conversions, and call volume
Use tools such as Google Analytics, Google Search Console, and GBP Insights
Analyze competitor performance in local search and identify growth opportunities
Cross-Functional Collaboration

Work with content, paid media, and reputation management teams to align strategies
Coordinate with customer service or operations teams for review and engagement workflows
Qualifications

3–5+ years of SEO experience, with a strong focus on local SEO
Proven experience managing and optimizing Google Business Profiles
Deep understanding of local ranking factors and Map Pack algorithms
Experience with SEO tools (Ahrefs, SEMrush, BrightLocal, Whitespark, etc.)
Strong analytical skills and ability to translate data into actionable insights
Experience with multi-location businesses is highly preferred
Nice to Have

Experience handling GBP suspensions and reinstatements
Familiarity with reputation management and review acquisition strategies
Knowledge of local service ads (LSAs) and how they complement SEO
Key Success Metrics

Increased Map Pack rankings and visibility
Growth in calls, direction requests, and website clicks from GBP
Improved local keyword rankings and organic traffic
Higher review volume and improved average rating
